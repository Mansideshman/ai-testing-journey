# Sahyadri Bank – Fixed Deposit Policy

## Interest Rates
- 6 months to 1 year: 6.5% per year
- 1 to 3 years: 7% per year
- 3 to 5 years: 7.25% per year
- Senior citizens (60+): extra 0.5%

## Deposit Amount
- Minimum deposit: ₹10,000

## Early Withdrawal
- Allowed, with a penalty of 1% on the interest rate
- No withdrawal allowed in the first 7 days
