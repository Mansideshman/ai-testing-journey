# Sahyadri Bank – Loan Policy

## Personal Loan
- Interest rate: 10.5% per year for salaried customers
- Interest rate: 12% per year for self-employed customers
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000
- Tenure: 1 to 5 years
- Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000

## Home Loan
- Interest rate: 8.5% per year
- Maximum tenure: 30 years
- Down payment: at least 20% of the property value

## Late EMI Payment
- Penalty: 2% of the EMI amount per month of delay
