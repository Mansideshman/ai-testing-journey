# Sahyadri Bank – Credit Card Policy

## Fees
- Annual fee: ₹500 (waived if you spend ₹50,000 or more in a year)
- Late payment fee: ₹750
- Cash withdrawal fee: 2.5% of the amount

## Eligibility
- Minimum age: 21 years
- Minimum monthly income: ₹20,000

## Credit Limit
- Starts at ₹50,000, based on income and credit history

## ATM withdrawal limit
- ₹40,000 per day
